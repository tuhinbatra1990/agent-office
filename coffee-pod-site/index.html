FAQ

Good to know.

Do Kōvo pods fit my machine?

Yes — our pods are designed to fit all standard single-serve pod machines. If yours takes a standard round pod, it takes ours.

Are the pods really compostable?

Every pod is made from plant-fiber material certified for home composting. Toss the whole pod, grounds and all, into your compost bin — no disassembly needed.

Can I change my flavor mix each month?

Absolutely. Log into your account before your renewal date to adjust your roast mix, delivery frequency, or shipping address.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel or pause your subscription anytime, no fees, no phone calls — it takes about 10 seconds in your account settings.
